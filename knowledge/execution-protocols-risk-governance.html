1. The Priority Ladder of Execution

In a live market environment, structural forces do not carry equal weight. A perfect technical base inflection means nothing if a binary earnings release triggers an unhedged 20% gap overnight. Therefore, the terminal processes setups through a strict Priority Invalidation Ladder before assigning an execution protocol:

Priority Institutional Filter Risk Mechanism Directive Behavior
1 (Highest) Binary Event Proximity Discontinuous price gap risk bypassing hard stops Hard Block or Compressed Runway
2 OpEx Expiration & Gamma Synthetic dealer pinning and range compression Anticipate strike pinning; void chase
3 Earnings Revision Drift Institutional consensus re-modeling velocity Tailor R-multiple targets based on revision age
4 Systematic Calendar Flows Passive 401(k) / retirement matching bids Expand baseline swing runway
5 (Baseline) Technical Price Action Calculated 2.0R asymmetry & pivot levels Standard momentum execution rules

2. Protocol Directory & Operational Mechanics

BLOCKED: Binary event blackout (<7d). Hard pass; overnight gap risk unhedged.
Associated Module: Macro Event & Binary Guardrails Trigger: Earnings within 0–7 sessions

Operational Mechanism: Stop-loss orders do not provide protection during earnings gaps because equities auction at discontinuous price levels before market open. Entering swing setups within 7 days introduces uncontrollable negative skew that destroys portfolio asymmetry. The protocol mandates an absolute trading block.

DEFENSIVE RUNWAY: Blackout in <14d. Scale out at +1.5R or exit before day 7.
Associated Module: Macro Event & Binary Guardrails Trigger: Earnings within 8–14 sessions

Operational Mechanism: Standard swing campaigns require multiple weeks to complete multi-leg expansions. When an upcoming reporting date caps the available holding period to under two weeks, the trade cannot mature. Risk must be managed by taking early partial profits at +1.5R and moving to 100% cash before day 7.

OPEX PINNING: Gamma suppression active. Expect range pinning at strike.
Associated Module: OpEx & Gamma Dynamics Trigger: OpEx countdown ≤ 3 sessions

Operational Mechanism: During expiration week, option dealers dynamic delta-hedging dampens realized volatility around strikes with large open interest. Breakouts attempted during this window frequently stall or form false-breakdown traps until options expire and gamma roll-overs resolve.

PEAK DIFFUSION: Fresh Rank 1 revision. Full swing leeway to 3.0R+ expansion.
Associated Module: Zacks Rank & Revisions Trigger: Rank #1 with tenure ≤ 3 days

Operational Mechanism: When Wall Street analysts revise earnings projections upward, institutional algorithms and multi-manager funds execute multi-day accumulation programs. A freshly upgraded #1 rank represents the maximum velocity of this Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEAD), justifying wider trailing stops to capture 3.0R+ moves.

MATURING DRIFT: Late Rank 2 drift. Trail tactical stop tightly along 10 EMA.
Associated Module: Zacks Rank & Revisions Trigger: Rank #2 with tenure ≥ 14 days

Operational Mechanism: Revision momentum decays exponentially. By day 14 to 20 following an initial revision cycle, institutional positioning is predominantly complete. Breakouts from late-stage drift carry higher failure rates; profits should be protected aggressively by ratcheting stops along the rising 10-day EMA.

TOM INFLOW TAILWIND: Automated allocations active. Standard trailing stop.
Associated Module: Turn-of-the-Month (TOM) Effect Trigger: Sessions between Day 29 and Day 3

Operational Mechanism: Automated 401(k) contributions, corporate matching, and sovereign pension allocations create an inelastic, price-insensitive bid on broad equities at the turn of each calendar month. Setups launched in this window benefit from structural tailwinds that reduce false-breakdown frequencies.

STANDARD RUNWAY: Full swing leeway. Void entry if opened >2.5% past pivot.
Associated Module: Execution Discipline & Slippage Rules Trigger: Clean horizon (>14d to earnings, normal OpEx & Zacks status)

Operational Mechanism: When no immediate exogenous calendar shocks are present, technical asymmetry rules govern execution. If an asset opens extended beyond 2.5% above the trigger pivot, the entry is automatically voided because the expanded stop distance destroys the required 2.0R asymmetry floor.

3. The Golden Rule: Cash as an Active Position

The systematic framework is designed to filter rather than generate volume. In corrective macro regimes (Amber/Red) or during heavy calendar clusters, the algorithm will intentionally output few or zero actionable setups. Maintaining cash during unfavorable structural windows is not an absence of strategy—it is a conscious risk posture designed to preserve capital for high-expectancy environments.
